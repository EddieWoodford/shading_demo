Sensor readings during the sweep

The tracer ramps the string voltage at a constant rate. Near short circuit the measured panel takes an equal share of that ramp with every panel that is not bypassed, so each bypassed panel makes its voltage climb faster. A shaded panel that is still producing shows up as a plateau: the current stalls at that panel's level while its voltage builds.

Simulated with pvlib (CEC single-diode module parameters, Bishop reverse-bias model) ·
